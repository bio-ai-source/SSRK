In the matched protocol of 20 bootstrap refits, SSRK's ARI (0.355) is significantly higher than that of CAE, GAEFS, and SSFS (and of Variance, nearly uninformative on this per-gene standardized matrix) and not significantly different from that of STG ($-0.009$, 95\% BCa interval $[-0.035,0.025]$); its NMI is not significantly different from that of SSFS ($-0.001$, Holm $p=0.68$), its coverage score is significantly higher than that of all five baselines, and its top-100 set is the most stable of the six methods (Jaccard index 0.772 against 0.756 for SSFS, marker Jaccard index 1.000; Appendix~\ref{sec:pbmc_app}). Among the baselines, STG has a higher NMI, and SSFS retrieves 0.8 more markers on average.
Table~\ref{tab:pbmc_stability} reports whole-set and marker stability over the 20 matched bootstrap refits; the indices are defined in Appendix~\ref{sec:metrics}, and pairwise indices are averaged over resamples. Every SSRK refit contains the same 14 markers from five lineage groups (marker hits 14 and coverage score 2.80 in all 20 refits), and SSRK has the highest whole-set Jaccard and Kuncheva indices of the six methods. Coordinatewise medians of $W$ over groups of refits define the consensus rankings of Table~\ref{tab:pbmc_stability}; consensus-versus-member comparisons measure within-group agreement, and consensus-versus-consensus comparisons use disjoint groups of refits. All consensus rankings retain the same 14 markers, and larger groups improve whole-set agreement.
